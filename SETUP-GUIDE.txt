MONEY TRACKER - SETUP GUIDE
===========================

FILES
- index.html, sw.js, manifest.json, icon-192.png, icon-512.png  -> the app (upload all together)
- Code.gs                                                        -> Google Sheets cloud sync (optional)

UPDATING FROM THE OLD VERSION
- Just replace the old files with these new ones (same link). Your data stays.
- If the phone still shows the old version, close the app and open it again.


STEP 1 - PUT THE APP ONLINE (free)
----------------------------------
GitHub Pages:
1. Create a free account at github.com
2. New repository (example: money-tracker), Public
3. "Add file" > "Upload files" > upload the 5 app files > Commit
4. Settings > Pages > Source: Deploy from a branch > main / (root) > Save
5. After 1-2 minutes: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/money-tracker/

Quick PC test only: double-click index.html (data stays in that browser only).


STEP 2 - INSTALL ON SAMSUNG A35 / PC
------------------------------------
Phone: open the link in Chrome > menu (3 dots) > Add to Home screen / Install app.
PC: open the link in Chrome/Edge > click the install icon in the address bar.


STEP 3 - CLOUD SYNC WITH GOOGLE SHEETS
--------------------------------------
1. sheets.google.com > blank spreadsheet (name: Money Tracker)
2. Extensions > Apps Script > delete sample code > paste Code.gs
3. Change SECRET_KEY to your own secret > Save
4. Deploy > New deployment > Web app
     Execute as: Me | Who has access: Anyone > Deploy > Authorize access
5. Copy the Web App URL (ends with /exec)
6. In the app: Settings > Cloud sync > paste URL + same secret key > Save settings
7. Do step 6 on your other device.

- Changes are saved on the device first (works offline), then synced when online.
- The Sheet shows tabs: Summary, Transactions, Accounts, Debts.
- Do not edit or delete the hidden "_data" tab.


DEBTS & LOANS (SPayLater, Pag-IBIG, SSS, etc.)
----------------------------------------------
Add:
- Debt page > + Add > choose the Type (SPayLater, GGives, Pag-IBIG Loan, SSS Loan,
  Credit Card, Home Credit, etc.) or type your own.
- Enter Total amount, Payment schedule (One-time, Weekly, Every 15 days, Monthly,
  Quarterly, Yearly), Amount per payment, No. of payments (optional), and Due date.
  If you enter the total and no. of payments, the amount per payment is computed for you.
- "I received this as cash in an account": ON for cash loans (Pag-IBIG MPL, SSS, GLoan),
  OFF for SPayLater / credit purchases (it turns off automatically for these types).

New SPayLater order / amount changed:
- Tap the debt > "+ Add" > enter the new amount and the NEW amount per payment.
  The total and monthly due update right away. History keeps every addition.
- You can also tap "Edit" anytime to change the amount per payment manually.

Pay:
- Tap the debt > Pay. It pre-fills the amount due this period. Choose the account and fee.
  The payment is deducted from your account and shown in your History.

Dashboard:
- Monthly dues = total of all your installments per month.
- Unpaid this period = what you still need to pay for the current month/period.
- Each card shows: Due date, Overdue (red), or Paid this period (green) + next due date.
- Filter by type using the chips (e.g., only SPayLater).


KEEPING YOUR DATA SAFE
----------------------
- Data is never deleted unless YOU delete it (Erase all data needs you to type DELETE).
- Do not clear browser/site data for the app. If it happens, cloud sync restores everything.
- Settings > Export backup (JSON) / Import backup. Export to Excel (CSV) is also available.
